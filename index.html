Area Guide — Greeley & Evans

Greeley, CO

  • Home to the University of Northern Colorado — brings culture, events, and a vibrant downtown
  • One of NoCo’s most affordable cities; median home price well below Fort Collins and Loveland
  • Strong healthcare infrastructure: Banner Health system, several clinics
  • Active manufactured home park community with multiple well-established parks
  • Access to US-34, US-85 corridors — commute-friendly to Fort Collins and Denver metro

Evans, CO

  • Directly adjacent to Greeley — shares most amenities with lower municipal fees
  • Several manufactured home parks concentrated near 37th St and W D St corridors
  • Weld County location: lower property taxes than Larimer County
  • Quick access to I-25 via US-34 — commuter-friendly to Loveland and Fort Collins
  • Family-friendly; smaller-town feel with proximity to full urban services next door

Why Manufactured Homes Here

  • Park density is high in Greeley/Evans — one of NoCo’s deepest MH markets
  • Newer homes (2015–2023) available well below $140K
  • $20K down positions you competitively on most listings in this range
  • Chattel financing available through specialized MH lenders — see Zachary’s lender page

Things to Consider

  • Lot rent is an ongoing monthly cost — ask Zachary for current park lot rent ranges before making an offer
  • Park rules vary: pet policies, subletting restrictions, and age requirements differ by community
  • Chattel loans (home-only) carry higher rates than traditional mortgages — review lender options carefully
  • Older homes (pre-1990) may have limited financing options; condition inspection is critical

5. Manufactured Home & Park-Specific Advisory

Manufactured homes located in land-lease (park) communities are subject to ongoing lot rent obligations, park rules, pet policies, age restrictions, subletting limitations, and other community regulations that vary by park. Buyers should request and carefully review the park’s lease agreement, rules and regulations, and lot rent history before submitting an offer. Financing for manufactured homes in parks typically involves chattel loans, which carry different terms, rates, and qualification standards than traditional mortgage products. Older homes (generally pre-HUD 1976, or pre-1990 depending on lender guidelines) may have limited or no financing options available. A professional inspection by a qualified manufactured home inspector is strongly recommended prior to purchase.
