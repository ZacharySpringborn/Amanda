Area Guide — Greeley & Evans

Greeley, CO

  • Home to the University of Northern Colorado — arts, culture, and a lively downtown core
  • One of NoCo’s most affordable cities; well below Fort Collins and Loveland pricing
  • Strong healthcare: Banner Health system and multiple clinics
  • Deep manufactured home park community across multiple well-established parks
  • US-34 / US-85 access — commute-friendly to Fort Collins and Denver metro

Evans, CO

  • Directly adjacent to Greeley — shares all major amenities with lower municipal fees
  • Concentrated MH parks near 37th St, W D St, and Buffalo Trail corridors
  • Weld County location: lower property taxes than Larimer County
  • Quick I-25 access via US-34 — commuter-friendly to Loveland and Fort Collins
  • Smaller-town feel with full urban services next door

Why Manufactured Homes Here

  • Greeley/Evans has one of NoCo’s deepest MH park markets — broad selection
  • Newer homes (2015–2023) available well under $140K
  • $20K down positions you competitively across most of these listings
  • Chattel financing available through specialized MH lenders — see lender resource

Things to Consider

  • Lot rent is an ongoing monthly cost — ask Zachary for current park rates before making an offer
  • Park rules vary: pets, subletting, and age restrictions differ by community
  • Chattel loans carry higher rates than traditional mortgages — review lender options carefully
  • Older homes (pre-1990) may have limited financing options; condition inspection is critical

5. Manufactured Home & Park-Specific Advisory

Manufactured homes located in land-lease (park) communities are subject to ongoing lot rent obligations, park rules, pet policies, age restrictions, subletting limitations, and other community regulations that vary significantly by park. Lot rent estimates are not included in this report; buyers should request and carefully review the park’s current lease agreement, rules and regulations, and lot rent history before submitting an offer. Park approval of the buyer may be required as a condition of sale. Financing for manufactured homes in parks typically involves chattel loans (home-only financing), which carry different terms, interest rates, down payment requirements, and qualification standards than traditional real property mortgage products. Older homes (generally pre-HUD 1976, or pre-1990 depending on lender guidelines) may have limited or no financing options available. A professional inspection by a qualified manufactured home inspector is strongly recommended prior to any purchase. Resale potential for manufactured homes in parks may be more limited than for site-built homes; buyers should consider long-term resale conditions. HUD certification year and compliance status should be independently verified.

6. Well & Septic Advisory

While manufactured homes in established parks typically connect to municipal water and sewer systems, buyers should independently verify all utility connections, water source, water quality, and the nature of any on-site septic systems if applicable.
